Common questions

Do you handle both exterior and interior work?

Yes. We build the structure and facade and complete flooring, ceilings, painting and modular interiors under one contract.

How long does a typical project take?

A new two-storey home usually takes 8 to 12 months. Interior-only projects take 6 to 12 weeks. You get a dated schedule before work starts.

Is the quote fixed?

We issue an itemised quote after the site visit. Any change you request is priced in writing before we proceed.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. A written warranty covers structure, waterproofing and finishes.
